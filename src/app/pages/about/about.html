About fitcalc

fitcalc is an independent project by Mazen Jlassi, a software developer. The goal is a small set of fast, private fitness calculators that explain their math instead of hiding it behind a form.

How content is produced

Formulas are implemented as tested functions and checked against the cited primary sources. Each calculator page states its formula, source, limitations, and the date it was last reviewed. Copy is written for this site; competitor text is not reused.

What this site is not

fitcalc is not a medical service and does not provide diagnosis or individualized treatment. Estimates can be wrong for many reasons, including measurement error and individual variation. Talk to a qualified professional about pregnancy, breastfeeding, adolescence, eating disorders, or any medical condition.

Privacy

Calculators run in your browser. There are no accounts and no analytics. Read the privacy policy for details.
