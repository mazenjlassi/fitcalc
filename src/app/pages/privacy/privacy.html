Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Calculations stay on your device

The numbers you enter into a calculator are processed in your browser. They are not sent to a server, stored in an account, or attached to an identifier. The site has no login and no calculator input is saved between visits.

Cookies and analytics

fitcalc does not set cookies and does not load analytics or advertising scripts at this time. When an ad or consent service is added, this section will name the provider, explain what is stored, and offer the required choices before any non-essential script runs.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like most hosting providers, it may process standard technical request data such as an IP address and user agent to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. That data is not used here to identify individuals.

Contact

Contact links may open your email client or a third-party code-hosting site. Those services have their own privacy policies. Do not send personal health information.
