The calorie calculator is being built. It will estimate basal metabolic rate, daily energy needs, and a sustainable target range using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.

What it will include

  • Metric and imperial input with accessible validation.
  • Activity factors from sedentary to extra active.
  • Maintain, mild deficit, and mild surplus goals with a minimum-intake warning.
  • The formula, source, accuracy limits, and a last-reviewed date.
